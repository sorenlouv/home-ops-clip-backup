Privacy policy

home-ops clip backup is used only by its owner, Søren Louv-Jansen, with his own Google account.

It asks Google for one permission, drive.file: access only to the files it creates itself. It copies files from the owner's home server to his Google Drive and deletes them after a year. It reads no other Drive files, stores no Google data anywhere else, and shares nothing with anyone.
